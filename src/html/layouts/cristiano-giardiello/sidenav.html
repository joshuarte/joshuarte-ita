<dr. Cristiano Giardiello from zero to HERO/>

Dr. Cristiano Giardiello immediately seemed to us a person with whom it would have been possible to collaborate, not just let us work as a standard operators. I mean, after an initial talking interview, having really tight deadlines, the doctor left us carte blanche in order to be able to continue processing by respecting budget and timing: we had contact on April 1st 2018 and from there in a month it would be aired the program "Una clinica per rinascere", on Realtime. As a result, the doctor needed a new identity, a logo, a website as a new information portal for his patients in less than a month.

We did not give up and the same evening we started working hard on his new identity. For the development, instead, we chose all open-source and free technologies, given the limited budget for the project.

We have completely revised all the textual content, laid the foundations for the new sitemap on the site, built and presented the new logo that immediately appealed to Doctor Giardiello, and presented the layout of the new website.

We must thank the doctor also for allowing us to enter the structure in which he operates, Pineta Grande, and exploit real places for the realization of a photoshooting that gave a completely different look to the graphics of the site thanks to photos of real and spontaneous professional life .

Furthermore, tools like Trello, Figma and the open-source libraries we used to write the site code were very useful to speed up our workflow, all managed through an Agile mentality shared between 3 people, directed by me.

In short, working for Dr. Giardiello was like entering a playground where we could unleash our creativity, while respecting the budget and tight deadlines.
